Adhesive Engineering

Choosing the right adhesive ensures your brand stays intact from the factory to the consumer's hands.

Environmental Resilience

We offer adhesives specialized for extreme environments where standard labels fail:

  • ❄️ Cold Temp: For refrigerated food and beverage storage.
  • 🔥 High Heat: For industrial electronics and battery casing.
  • 💧 High Moisture: For bathroom, luxury spa, and shower products.

Surface Compatibility

We match the label tack to your specific surface material for 100% adherence:

  • 📦 Corrugated: High-tack for rough, porous paper surfaces.
  • 🧴 Glass/Plastic: Clean removal options for reusable packaging.
  • 📐 Tight Mandrel: For small diameter curves like tubes and vials.
