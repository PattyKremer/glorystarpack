Everything You Need to Know

New to custom packaging? We've answered the most common questions from our global B2B partners.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)? +
Our standard MOQ starts at 100 pcs for rigid and magnetic gift boxes. For mailers and bags, it's 200-300 pcs. We support scaling brands.
How long does production and shipping take? +
Standard production is 12-18 days after sample approval. Air shipping takes 3-5 days, while sea freight takes 18-25 days depending on destination.
